Dear Hiring Team,

I am writing to express my motivation for the Business Analysis and Testing Coordination role. With 8+ years in software quality engineering, I combine requirements clarification, stakeholder collaboration, and hands-on coordination of testing across the full project lifecycle — most recently as QA Lead in banking at Empyrean DC Ireland Limited.

At Empyrean (Dec 2022 – Present), I lead a team of five QA Engineers across two enterprise banking applications. I define QA strategy, test planning, and release readiness for multiple Agile squads; review test plans, cases, and execution reports; and coordinate sprint planning, backlog refinement, defect triage, and release planning. I collaborate closely with Product Owners, Business Analysts, Developers, and Project Managers to align business needs with testable outcomes, and I provide clear status, risks, and release recommendations. Through stronger process and triage, I have reduced defect leakage by 25% and defect resolution time by 30%. I also perform end-to-end ETL and data-pipeline validation (Azure Databricks, Snowflake), including SQL-based checks of transformation logic and source-to-target completeness — supporting data mapping and data-quality collaboration with technical teams.

Previously, at Aruba Networks (Hewlett Packard Enterprise), I led end-to-end QA for Agile releases on enterprise networking applications: analysing requirements with Product Owners and Business Analysts, identifying gaps early, defining test strategies, coordinating deliverables across Scrum teams, and supporting production and hotfix releases with smoke, sanity, and regression testing. I validated backends (Amazon RDS, DynamoDB, InfluxDB) and APIs (Postman), and tracked defects through Azure DevOps.

At Cognizant Technology Solutions, working in a Waterfall delivery model, I analysed business requirements, clarified gaps with the on-site QA lead, maintained requirements-to-test traceability, and executed functional, integration, regression, end-to-end, and UAT testing, including SQL backend validation and defect management in HP ALM. I was recognised as Key Contributor of the Quarter for contribution to product quality.

I am motivated by roles that connect business intent to reliable delivery in financial services and regulated environments. I bring solid Agile experience (user stories, acceptance criteria), Waterfall lifecycle exposure, strong organisational and communication skills, and familiarity with Jira, Azure DevOps, and related collaboration tools. I would welcome the opportunity to support your stakeholders with structured analysis, coordinated testing, and clear alignment across business and technical teams.

Yours sincerely,
